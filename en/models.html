Transparency & security

Your rights

Your likeness stays yours.

Swiss law

Full legal transparency

Our model contract is drafted under Swiss law (Art. 28 ZGB). You do not cede any copyright — only defined usage rights for specific contexts. Everything is written clearly and in English.

Defined limits

You decide what your image is used for

In the contract we define together: no use for alcohol, tobacco, political content or anything else you do not agree to. These limits are contractually binding. No exceptions.

Right of withdrawal

Opt-out possible at any time

You can withdraw your consent, with a contractually defined transition period. No penalties, no contractual fines. We handle it fairly and transparently.

Data protection

Your data remains protected

Your personal data and photos are used exclusively for AI training and booking administration. No sharing with third parties. Compliant with the Swiss Data Protection Act (nFADP).

Definition

What is AI brand imagery?

AI brand imagery is brand-specific, AI-generated imagery — the opposite of generic stock. It is produced exclusively for a brand and guarantees a consistent visual style across all channels: website, social media, print, annual report, recruiting.

Visari curates and produces these visual worlds in Zurich — either with selected AI Models from our pool or with an exclusive Branded Model that exists only for your brand. Legally compliant under Swiss law (Art. 28 ZGB), EU AI Act-compliantly documented.

Exclusivity

The Branded Model — your exclusive brand face

A Branded Model works like a logo — only visual. It is an AI-generated face that belongs exclusively to your brand. Permanently available, deployable across all campaigns and channels, season-independent and consistent. Nobody else uses this face.

The Branded Model becomes the visual anchor of your communication: a permanent brand asset that creates recognition without relying on real people. Contract negotiations, availability bottlenecks and model changes become a thing of the past.

Frequently Asked Questions

Your questions about AI brand imagery

What distinguishes AI brand imagery from stock photos?

Stock photos are generic and multi-licensed — the same image appears on competitor websites. AI brand imagery is produced exclusively for your brand: consistent style, recognisable face, unified visual language across all channels.

What is a Branded Model?

A Branded Model is an AI-generated brand face that belongs exclusively to your brand. Like a logo, but visual — permanently available, deployable across all campaigns and channels, never used for other brands.

Can we integrate our existing branding?

Yes. During the briefing, we jointly define your brand's colour world, tonality, visual language and style anchors. The AI brand imagery is seamlessly integrated into your existing brand world.

How many images does a typical brand imagery package include?

Depending on needs, 30–100+ final images. Many clients start with a test package (10–20 images) and then expand as needed.

Is AI brand imagery suitable for the annual report?

Yes, provided it is labelled as AI-generated. Visari delivers all images EU AI Act-compliantly labelled with complete licence documentation.

What does an AI brand imagery package cost?

Depending on scope, a few thousand francs — a fraction of a traditional campaign shoot. Specific quotes after briefing.

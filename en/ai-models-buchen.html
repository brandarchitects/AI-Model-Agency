Frequently Asked Questions

What You Should Know About AI Models.

What are AI Models?

AI Models are AI-generated person images used for commercial purposes such as advertising, content and brand identity. Unlike traditional models, they require no physical photoshoot. They are based on either fully synthetic constructs (Synthetic Origin) or licensed likenesses of real individuals (Human Origin). At Visari in Zurich, all models are curated and reviewed by experienced creative directors before entering the pool. The result is studio-quality images available in 3–5 days.

How do AI Models differ from stock photos?

The biggest difference is exclusivity. Stock photos on platforms like Getty, Shutterstock or Adobe Stock can be used by hundreds of companies simultaneously — your competitor may be using the same image. AI Models from Visari are exclusively bookable for your brand. That means: consistent visual language across all campaigns, no risk of confusion, and full control over context, style and usage. You also receive complete license documentation that stock platforms do not provide in this form.

Are AI-generated images legally compliant in Switzerland?

Yes, completely. At Visari, all images are licensed and documented under Swiss law (Art. 28 ZGB, personality rights). Additionally, we label all images as AI-generated in compliance with the EU AI Act. You receive complete license documentation to present to your legal department: scope of use, consent proof (for Human Origin), labeling guidelines and contract duration. This keeps you legally protected in Switzerland and throughout the EU/EEA.

How much do AI Models cost in Switzerland?

Significantly less than a traditional photoshoot. A typical advertising shoot in Switzerland costs between CHF 8,000 and CHF 25,000 when you include the photographer, studio, models, styling and post-production. AI-generated campaigns at Visari start at a few hundred francs, depending on scope and desired exclusivity. The savings are typically 70–90%, with faster delivery (3–5 days instead of 4–8 weeks). Contact us for an individual quote.

Which industries are AI Models suitable for?

AI Models are suitable for practically all industries that need professional people photography. They are particularly popular in retail and e-commerce (product images with people), financial services and insurance (campaigns, employer branding), technology and pharma (corporate communication), hospitality and tourism, as well as creative and digital agencies looking to expand their services. Demand is particularly high in Switzerland, Germany and Austria.

Can I have an exclusive AI Model developed for my brand?

Yes. With a Branded Model from Visari, you receive a fully exclusive AI Model as a permanent brand asset. This model is used exclusively for your brand and appears in no other campaign. It is available indefinitely — for every season, every channel and every format. Comparable to a brand ambassador, but without ongoing contracts, scheduling or availability issues. Ideal for companies that want to build a consistent visual identity over many years.

Yes. All AI-generated images from Visari are delivered by default in print-ready resolution (300 DPI, CMYK-compatible). They are suitable for large-format posters, brochures, catalogues, packaging, trade show materials and all other physical media. You also receive the images in digital formats (RGB, various aspect ratios) for online campaigns, social media and e-commerce. One image package covers all channels.

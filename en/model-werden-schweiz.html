Becoming a model in Switzerland — classic or modern?

Modelling in Switzerland has two paths. The classic route goes through agencies with comp cards, castings and photoshoots. The modern route is AI modelling: one-time onboarding from home, no photoshoot, passive income. This guide compares both honestly.

The classic route

Classic modelling in Switzerland

If you want to model the classic way, you typically need a minimum height (175 cm women, 185 cm men), clothing size 34–38, a well-groomed appearance and often set experience. Entry is through castings, comp card submissions or street casting. Well-known Swiss agencies include Streetcasting, Prime Models, Hero Model, Faces Models and The-Models.

Earnings per booking range from CHF 500 to CHF 5,000+, but most models only have 5–20 bookings per year. Travel to set, exclusivity contracts and long waiting periods are common.

The modern route

AI modelling at Visari — the new path

AI modelling works differently: you take 5 photos at home following a briefing, one time only. Visari trains an AI model of your face from these. After that, images are generated — without you ever having to stand in front of a camera again.

There are no height requirements, no age limits, no set appointments. What counts: charisma and naturalness. Earnings follow a revenue share model: CHF 225 to CHF 3,250 per booking, plus a CHF 100 first-booking bonus. Passive, automatic, transparent.

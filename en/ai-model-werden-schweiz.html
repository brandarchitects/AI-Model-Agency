Basics

What Is an AI Model?

The term sounds technical, but it's simple: an AI Model is an AI-generated representation of a person used for commercial advertising. Modelling with Visari means: no studio, no casting, no appointment. At Visari, there are two types:

Synthetic Origin

A fully synthetic AI Model. No real person serves as a basis. The appearance is generated entirely by AI.

Human Origin

The AI Model is based on the licensed likeness of a real person — you. Your photos serve as training data. The generated image resembles you, but is not a direct photograph.

As a registered Visari Model, you work in the Human Origin category. Your real appearance is captured photographically once, used to create an AI model, and the original photos are then deleted. What remains is a digital model that we use exclusively for curated, reputable advertising purposes.

Transparency & Security

Your Appearance Remains Yours.

The biggest concern is loss of control. We take that seriously. Here, without any marketing spin, is exactly what the contract covers.

Usage Limits

You decide how your image is used

We jointly establish in the contract which industries and content types are excluded. No use for alcohol, tobacco, political or erotic content. Contractually binding.

Withdrawal

Exit at any time

You can revoke your consent at any time. A contractually defined transition period applies for ongoing campaigns. No penalties.

Privacy

Your photos are deleted

Original photos are used solely for training and then deleted. What remains is the trained digital model. Compliant with Swiss nDSG.

Freedom

No exclusivity contract

You can work in parallel for other clients, agencies or projects. Visari makes no claims on your future activities.

What you do NOT give up: Your copyright, your identity, your right to your name, your personality rights under Art. 28 ZGB.

What you DO grant: Defined, time-limited and content-limited usage rights to your likeness for commercial AI-generated images.

Open Questions

What Interests Most People.

"What if my face appears somewhere I'm not comfortable with?"

Every booking is reviewed by Visari before approval. Clients must disclose the campaign and intended use. Anything not specified in the contract is not approved. All AI-generated images must be labeled as such (EU AI Act).

"Can I see when my model gets booked?"

Yes. You are notified by email for every booking, including the client name and usage context. Full transparency is a fundamental principle of our business.

"What happens if my appearance changes?"

The AI model is created once and remains as-is. Changes to your appearance have no impact on the existing model. You can request an update at any time or revoke your consent.

"Can I be identified by my real name?"

No. Your name is not shared with clients. The AI model has its own neutral model name. Your identity remains protected.

"What if Visari ceases to exist?"

Covered by contract: complete deletion of all data and models upon closure of the business. No resale to third parties.

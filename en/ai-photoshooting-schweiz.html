Definition

What is an AI Photoshooting?

An AI photoshooting replaces the traditional photoshoot with AI-generated images. Instead of a photographer, studio, models and post-production, Visari delivers finished campaign images directly from the AI pipeline — in days rather than weeks, at a fraction of the cost.

The images are based on curated AI Models from the Visari pool or on an exclusive Branded Model for your brand. Every image is labelled as AI-generated (EU AI Act-compliant) and delivered with complete licence documentation under Swiss law.

Legal Compliance

Legally compliant — even without a real photoshoot

Every AI photoshooting at Visari is based on licensed individuals with documented consent under Art. 28 ZGB. The images have clear copyright attribution and are authorised for commercial use.

From August 2026, the EU AI Act requires labelling of AI-generated images for commercial use in the EU/EEA area. Visari labels all deliveries compliantly by default — you don't need to worry about a thing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Your Questions About AI Photoshooting

How does an AI photoshooting differ from a traditional photoshoot?

For a traditional photoshoot you need a photographer, studio, models, styling and post-production — typically CHF 8,000–25,000 and 4–8 weeks. With an AI photoshooting, Visari delivers finished images from the AI pipeline: 3–5 days, from a few hundred francs, with complete licence documentation.

Are AI photoshooting images suitable for print?

Yes. We deliver in 300 DPI print-ready resolution — suitable for posters, magazines, annual reports and all common print formats.

Can I use the same model for multiple campaigns?

Yes — this is one of the greatest advantages. With a Branded Model or a pool model, you can run as many campaigns as you like without organising a new photoshoot each time.

Is an AI photoshooting legally compliant?

Yes. All Visari images are based on licensed individuals with documented consent under Art. 28 ZGB. We also provide EU AI Act-compliant labelling and complete licence documentation.

Which industries is an AI photoshooting suitable for?

For all industries with regular image needs: insurance, banking, pharma, e-commerce, retail, technology, services. Especially suited for brands that need consistent visual language across multiple campaigns.

Can I use my own team as AI references?

Yes. With the consent of the respective person, Visari can train employees as a Human-Origin model. This is particularly suited for corporate communication when authentic team images are desired.

Definition

What is an AI Image Stock?

An AI Image Stock is a curated collection of AI-generated images produced exclusively for a brand. Unlike stock photo platforms such as Adobe Stock, Getty Images or Shutterstock, these images are not multi-licensed — they belong to your brand. In Switzerland, Visari, based in Zurich, specialises in such AI Image Stocks.

We develop bespoke visual worlds for brands, corporates and SMEs: consistent across all channels, legally compliant under Swiss law and EU AI Act, deliverable in 3-5 business days. No stock platform account, no AI tool subscription — a curated product.

Legal Compliance

Legal framework — what you need to know about AI Image Stocks

Swiss Law (Art. 28 ZGB): Personality-rights protection for people in the image stock. Visari holds a documented licence for every person. Your brand receives clearly defined usage rights.

EU AI Act (from August 2026): Labelling obligation for AI images in commercial use in the EU/EEA. Visari labels all delivered images compliantly by default and provides the corresponding documentation.

Copyright (URG): As the client, you receive exclusive commercial usage rights for the agreed context. Visari retains the technological rights to the training model.

Frequently Asked Questions

Your Questions About AI Image Stock

What is the difference between AI Image Stock and AI Model?

An AI Image Stock is a collection of AI-generated images for diverse applications (web, social, print, annual report). An AI Model is a recognisable AI person used as a brand ambassador across all campaigns. Many brands combine both.

Can I expand the AI Image Stock after it has been delivered?

Yes. Once style and models are defined, Visari can produce additional motifs in the same look at any time. This is one of the greatest advantages over traditional photoshoots.

Who owns the images in the AI Image Stock?

As the client, you receive exclusive commercial usage rights as per contract. Visari retains the technological rights to the training model.

Are AI images allowed in the annual report?

Yes, provided they are labelled as AI-generated. The EU AI Act requires this from August 2026 for commercial use in the EU/EEA area. Visari delivers all images with the corresponding labelling and licence documentation.

How many images does a typical AI Image Stock contain?

30-100 final images per image stock is typical, depending on the brief. Some clients start with a small test stock (10-20 images), others build a large library directly.

What happens if I want to change an image stock style?

If the brand world evolves, a new image stock style can be defined. The original image stock remains usable. For major brand refreshes, a parallel new build is often recommended instead of a migration.

Can brand-owned employees also serve as AI references?

Yes — this is a common request, especially from SMEs. With the consent of the respective person according to Art. 28 ZGB, Visari pipelines can train the person as a Human-Origin model. Note: clear internal regulations are required for such a setup, and we advise on this in the briefing.

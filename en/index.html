The process

From inquiry to finished image.

No casting, no shoot dates, no production weeks. The path to your final images is short and fully managed by us.

01

Inquiry & briefing

You describe your campaign, your brand or your image-library needs: tone, target audience, channels, desired image style. We propose matching models.

02

Selection & licensing

You choose one or more models. We clarify availability, exclusivity and usage rights. Everything legally compliant under Swiss law.

03

Production

First we deliver the source material: avatar including prompt list, set card and sample images. Optionally we produce the images or stock material you need: 8–15 final images in your defined campaign context.

04

Delivery in 3–5 days

Final images in print-ready and digital resolution. Licence documentation as agreed and within legal bounds included.

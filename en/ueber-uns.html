Who we are

We are Brand Architects, your branding boutique for Switzerland. As a focused network of seasoned specialists, we develop brands that stand out and sell. Efficient, balanced and 100% creative.

Through our daily work with brands and campaigns, we identified a clear need: professional, consistent imagery featuring people — flexible, fast and legally compliant. Visari was born out of that need.

As an AI model agency, we combine our branding know-how with state-of-the-art image generation. The result: a curated pool of synthetic and licensed models that closes the gap between stock photography and traditional agencies.

About Visari

The people behind Visari.

Visari is not an anonymous project. Behind every model, every contract and every campaign stands an experienced team rooted in Swiss branding — for over 20 years.

Who we are

We are Brand Architects, an established branding boutique from Zurich. As a focused network of experienced specialists, we have been developing brands that stand out and sell for over 20 years.

Through our daily work with brands and campaigns, we identified a clear need: professional, consistent imagery featuring real people — flexible, fast and legally compliant. Visari was born out of that need.

As an AI model agency, we combine our branding know-how with state-of-the-art image generation. The result: a curated pool of synthetic and licensed models that closes the gap between stock photography and traditional agencies.

In the day-to-day running of Visari, Simone, Enrique and Pascal are the permanent contacts. For larger campaigns, the Brand Architects network joins in — specialists in strategy, design and production whom we bring on board per project.

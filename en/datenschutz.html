Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2025

1. Controller

Brand Architects Design GmbH
(operating under the brand name "Visari")
Switzerland
Email: info@visari.ch

2. Scope

This privacy policy applies to the website visari.ch and all associated subpages. It provides information on the collection, processing and use of personal data in accordance with the Swiss Data Protection Act (nFADP, in force since 1 September 2023) and the European General Data Protection Regulation (EU GDPR), to the extent applicable.

3. Data collected

3.1 When visiting the website

When accessing our website, the following technical data is automatically recorded:

  • IP address (anonymised)
  • Date and time of access
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referrer URL
  • Pages visited

This data is processed to ensure the smooth operation of the website and to improve our service. No assignment to a specific person takes place. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR / Art. 31 nFADP).

3.2 When contacting us (email)

If you contact us by email, we process:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • The content of your message

This data is used exclusively to process your inquiry and is not passed on to third parties. Legal basis: contract initiation or legitimate interest.

3.3 When applying as a model

If you apply as a model, we process:

  • Your name, age and contact details
  • The photos you submit
  • Additional information you voluntarily provide

This data is used exclusively to review your application and — upon acceptance — to perform the contractual relationship. In the event of rejection, photos are deleted within 30 days unless otherwise agreed. Legal basis: contract initiation (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

4. Cookies and tracking

Our website currently does not use cookies or tracking tools. Should this change, we will update this privacy policy accordingly and — where required by law — obtain your consent.

5. Hosting and data processing

Our website is hosted via Vercel Inc. (USA). Data may therefore be transferred to the USA. Vercel has committed to comply with appropriate data protection standards. Legal basis for the transfer: legitimate interest in operating the website and Standard Contractual Clauses (SCC) in accordance with the EU Commission.

6. Transfer of data to third parties

In principle, we do not pass on your personal data to third parties unless:

  • You have expressly consented
  • There is a legal obligation
  • The transfer is necessary for the performance of the contract (e.g. to hosting providers)

7. Data security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. Transmission takes place in encrypted form (HTTPS/TLS).

8. Retention period

Personal data is only stored for as long as necessary for the respective processing purpose or as long as there are statutory retention obligations. After the deadline has expired, the data is routinely deleted.

9. Your rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access: You can request information about the data we have stored at any time.
  • Right to rectification: You can request the correction of incorrect data.
  • Right to erasure: You can request the deletion of your data, provided no statutory retention obligations conflict with this.
  • Restriction of processing: You can request the restriction of the processing of your data.
  • Data portability: You can request the release of your data in a common format.
  • Right to object: You can object to the processing of your data at any time.
  • Withdrawal of consent: You can withdraw consent given at any time.

To exercise your rights, please contact: info@visari.ch

10. Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority:

  • Switzerland: Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), www.edoeb.admin.ch
  • EU/EEA: The competent data protection supervisory authority in your country of residence

11. Changes to this privacy policy

We reserve the right to adapt this privacy policy at any time to reflect changes in the legal situation or in our data processing. The version published on our website at the relevant time shall apply.

12. Special notes on AI image generation

As part of our service, AI-generated images are created that are based on licensed likenesses of real persons or on fully synthetic models. The processing of biometric data (photos) takes place exclusively with the express consent of the persons concerned and within the framework of a specific licence agreement under Swiss law (Art. 28 ZGB). All generated images are labelled as AI-generated in accordance with the EU AI Act.

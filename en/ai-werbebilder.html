Definition

What are AI Advertising Images?

AI advertising images are professional, AI-generated photographs for commercial use. Not stock, not self-service tools — a curated, professional service. Visari produces campaign-ready imagery with real-looking AI Models, in any context, for any channel.

From idea to delivery, Visari handles the entire production: briefing, model selection, AI generation, quality control and licence documentation. You receive finished images — legally compliant, EU AI Act-compliant and ready for immediate use.

Frequently Asked Questions

Your questions about AI advertising images

Are AI advertising images suitable for print media?

Yes. We deliver in 300 DPI print-ready resolution — suitable for posters, magazines, annual reports and all common print formats.

Can I use the same model for different campaigns?

Yes. With a pool model or Branded Model you can run as many campaigns as you like, always with the same face.

How do AI advertising images differ from AI image stock?

AI advertising images are individual, campaign-specific productions. An AI image stock is a larger, structured image collection for diverse applications. Many clients combine both.

Are the images legally safe for commercial use?

Yes. All images are based on licensed individuals, documented under Art. 28 ZGB and labelled in compliance with the EU AI Act.

Can agencies order AI advertising images for their clients?

Yes. Visari offers white-label solutions and volume pricing for creative agencies. Get in touch.
